Turning one long idea into a publishable X presence has three very different routes: hand-split it yourself, publish it as a single long-form Article, or ask a general AI chatbot to "make it a thread." MD2X's [thread splitter](/thread) is a fourth, narrower tool. This piece draws the boundaries between them, based on X's own Articles help and MD2X's documentation as of 2026-10-23.

## The four approaches

**Manual splitting.** You write the draft in a notes app, cut it into posts, number them, and copy each one. Total editorial control, zero tooling — but repeated, and easy to get inconsistent breaks or numbering.

**X Articles.** X's own long-form format (help.x.com/en/using-x/articles) is for publishing a single, continuous long piece, not a broken-up numbered sequence. It uses X's rich-text editor.

**General AI chatbot.** Paste your draft into a chatbot and ask it to "split this into a thread." It can reason about where to break, but it is not wired to your clipboard, your local files, or consistent numbering — and it may reword your text.

**MD2X thread splitter.** You paste Markdown into the browser and the tool splits it at paragraph boundaries into numbered posts, with an optional AI-assisted split, then copies each post or the whole thread to your clipboard. No draft is uploaded unless you explicitly use an AI feature.

## When to use which

- **Use the MD2X splitter** when you already wrote the prose and only need a clean, numbered, copy-ready break-up — without rewording.
- **Use X Articles** when the piece reads better as one long-form article than as a sequence of posts.
- **Split manually** when each post needs individual editorial judgment, nuanced ordering, or custom punchlines between them.
- **Use a general chatbot** only as a second-opinion brainstorm, not as your final pipeline.

## Where the tool does not reach

- MD2X does not post to X for you; it prepares the clipboard content. You still paste and publish.
- The AI-assisted split is optional and requires your own OpenAI-compatible API key; the default rule-based split needs no key.
- It is tuned for thread-length posts, not for producing a single long-form Article — that's what X Articles is for.

## Boundaries

A thread and an Article solve different distribution needs: a numbered sequence invites swipe/reply momentum, while a single article keeps a reader in one document. MD2X sits between your draft and the clipboard; it does not host content, does not require an account, and does not change your words beyond deciding where the breaks go. Verify current X Articles formatting limits on X's help center.
